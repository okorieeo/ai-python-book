== 5. Who gets paid features? The same subscription at different moments
active                          day 5  -> PAID active
trialing                        day 5  -> PAID free trial
active, cancel at period end    day 20 -> PAID cancels at the end of the period
past_due, day 1 of grace        day 1  -> PAID payment failed, in the grace period
past_due, day 4 (grace over)    day 4  -> free payment failed and the grace period is over
canceled                        day 5  -> free the subscription was canceled
unpaid                          day 5  -> free the invoice stayed unpaid
incomplete                      day 5  -> free the first payment is not complete
paused                          day 5  -> free the subscription is paused
