# Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA): TEMPLATE

Do one when processing is likely to put people at high risk: sensitive data at scale, profiling, new technology,
children, or monitoring. Chat products that invite health or financial confidences usually qualify. Check your regulator's form (the NDPC publishes a DPIA schedule in the GAID).

1. **Describe the processing**: what data, from whom, how collected, where it flows, who sees it, how long it is kept.
2. **Why it is needed**: purpose, legal basis, why less data would not work.
3. **Consultation**: who you asked (users, security, legal, DPO).
4. **Risks to people** (not to the company): list each with likelihood (low/medium/high) and severity.
   Examples: leak of conversations; AI gives harmful advice; biased treatment; function creep; re-identification.
5. **Measures**: for each risk, the control, the owner, and the residual risk.
6. **Sign-off**: DPO or owner decision, date, review date. If high risk remains, ask the regulator before you start.
