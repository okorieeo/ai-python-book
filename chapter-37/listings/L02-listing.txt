== 5. Who gets paid features? Dates, not statuses
active                             day 10 -> PAID active
active, trial running              day 10 -> PAID free trial
active, day 1 after due date       day 31 -> PAID renewal payment pending, in the grace period
active, day 4 after due date       day 34 -> free the renewal payment was not received
attention (last charge failed)     day 20 -> PAID last renewal failed, update your card
attention, day 4 after due date    day 34 -> free the renewal payment was not received
non-renewing, before the date      day 20 -> PAID cancelled, active until the end of the period
non-renewing, after the date       day 31 -> free the subscription has ended
cancelled                          day 20 -> free the subscription was cancelled
completed                          day 20 -> free the subscription is complete
