# Product brief: AI Chat Assistant for Small Business Owners

## Problem
Small shop owners lose hours each week writing customer replies, product
descriptions and social posts. Existing AI tools feel complicated and have
confusing pricing.

## Target user
Amaka-type persona: runs a small online shop, works mostly on her phone,
pays with a local card or bank transfer, mobile data is limited.

## Solution (one paragraph)
A simple chat assistant, web and mobile, with ready-made prompts for common
shop tasks, clear monthly plans with a visible usage meter, and payment by card
or bank transfer.

## MVP scope (must have)
Sign up and login, streaming chat, conversation history, plans with token
limits, subscription payments (Stripe and Paystack), web app.

## Not in the MVP
Teams, document Q&A, voice, plugins, mobile app (follows after web launch).

## Success metrics (first 90 days)
- 100 sign-ups; 40% send a message within 24 hours
- 10% of active users upgrade to a paid plan
- Under 1% failed payments caused by our bugs; p95 first token under 2 s

## Biggest risks
AI cost per user, payment webhook reliability, users who never come back.
