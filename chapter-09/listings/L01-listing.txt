When I have to answer a long customer message,
I want to paste it and get a polite draft reply,
so I can respond in two minutes instead of twenty.

When my free tokens run out in the middle of a task,
I want to understand what happened and what it costs to continue,
so I can decide without losing my work.

When a payment fails,
I want to be told clearly why and how to fix it,
so I do not lose access to my conversations.
